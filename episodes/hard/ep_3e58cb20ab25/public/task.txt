The books may be clean. Split payments, partial payments, refunds, unidentified receipts and recurring equal amounts can be legitimate. Hard mode: the ledger is larger; line items keyed in by hand (some invoices, re-issues and duplicate copies) may be re-typed (other order, case and punctuation of the descriptions) with the same products, quantities and prices; an invoice cancelled by a full credit note and re-issued with the same line items is legitimate.
